## Research Overview

My research focuses on the structure and representation theory of **quantum supergroups**, with connections to quantum integrable systems, mathematical physics, and low-dimensional topology. Specifically, I study explicit equivalences among different realizations (Drinfeld–Jimbo, new Drinfeld, and RLL) of finite and affine quantum superalgebras, as well as the construction of their universal and evaluated *R*-matrices.
